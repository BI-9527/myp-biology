MYP unit

Unit overview

Statement of inquiry

Energy is never lost: it changes from one form to another, and the energy sources we choose change the world we live in.

Key conceptChange
Related conceptsEnergy · Transformation · Consequences
Global contextGlobalization and sustainability: how we use and share natural resources
ATL skillsThinking (critical thinking) · Social (collaboration) · Research (information literacy) · Communication

Inquiry questions

Factual: What are kinetic and potential energy? Which energy sources are renewable?

Conceptual: What makes energy change from one form to another? Why is some energy always wasted?

Debatable: Should Lebanon get all of its electricity from renewable sources?

Assessment in this unit

📝 Formative quiz after Station 3 · 🧪 Lab report in Station 5 · 🎯 GRASPS task in Station 6 · 🎓 Summative test at the end.

Teams

Name the four teams. Points are kept on this computer across all six stations.

How to play

  • Project the station page. Students write in their student sheet: question numbers match.
  • 🧍 Stand-up: stand for TRUE, sit for FALSE when the countdown ends.
  • ⚔️ Battle: each team agrees, then shows 1–4 fingers. Tap the teams that were right.
  • 🔎 Hunts and challenges: a team sends one player to the board.
  • Use + / − on the scoreboard for great answers and teamwork.
  • Tools at the top: ⏱ timer, 🎲 random pick, A+ bigger text, ⛶ full screen. Esc closes activities.
