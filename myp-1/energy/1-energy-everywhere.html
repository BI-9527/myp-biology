What is energy?

Energy is what makes things happen. It makes things move, heat up, light up, make sound, and grow. You cannot see energy itself, but you can see what it does.

Q1.2 Brain teaser: Which two cards store energy because of something's position or shape, so the energy is "waiting" to be used? Why do we call them potential?
Gravitational potential (high up) and elastic potential (stretched or squashed). Potential means "could happen": the energy is stored and can be released later, when the object falls or springs back.

Q1.4 Brain teaser: A book sits still on the top shelf. Does it have energy? How could you prove it?
Yes: gravitational potential energy, because it is high up. If it falls, that stored energy becomes kinetic energy (and a loud sound when it hits the floor!). Stored energy is still energy.
Q1.5 Real world: Your phone is on 10%. Which energy store is in the battery? Name three things that energy turns into when you play a game.
The battery stores chemical energy. Playing a game turns it into light (screen), sound (speaker) and thermal energy (the phone gets warm), passing through electrical energy on the way.
