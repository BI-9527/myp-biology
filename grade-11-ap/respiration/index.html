Mission: Power the Cell

Lara is running the Beirut Marathon: 42 km through the city. Every step costs her muscles energy in the form of ATP. Where does it come from?

Your teams are the cell's energy engineers. Over six missions you will follow one molecule of glucose from Lara's breakfast man'oushe all the way to ATP, and earn ATP points for your team on the way.

How to run a session

  • Project the session page. Students follow in their printed booklet: the question numbers match.
  • 🧍 Stand-up: everyone stands for TRUE and sits for FALSE when the countdown ends.
  • ⚔️ Quiz Battle: each team agrees, then holds up 1–4 fingers. Tap the teams that were right to award ATP.
  • 🎭 Act it out: students become molecules. Use 🎲 Pick to choose who performs.
  • Use + / − on the scoreboard at the bottom for anything else: good answers, teamwork, tidy booklets.
  • Press Mission complete at the end of each page to power up the class meter.
  • Tools at the top: ⏱ timer, 🎲 random pick, A+ bigger text, ⛶ full screen. Esc closes any full-screen activity.
