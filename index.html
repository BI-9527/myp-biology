Send one part at a time

The unit is split into seven pages. Give a class the link to the part they are working on; that page links backwards and to the glossary, but the parts you have not reached yet appear locked.

How the locks work, honestly

The tabs are a guide for students, not a security wall. Anything published to this site can be opened by anyone who types the address. If a part must stay private until a set date, keep its file out of the repository until then — that is the only real lock.

Suggested release order

  1. Share Overview in the first lesson, or project it yourself.
  2. Share each session at the start of that lesson. Students can still open earlier sessions to revise.
  3. Share Glossary once, at the beginning — it is reference material and never gives anything away.
  4. Share Check, reflect and export at the end. It gathers everything students typed in every session into one file to hand in.

Things worth knowing

  • Work students type is saved in their own browser on their own device. It does not reach you until they use the export button.
  • Because every page sits on the same address, answers typed in Session 1 are still there in Session 4 and appear in the final export.
  • Every page works with no internet once loaded, and prints as a paper worksheet.
  • The whole unit on one page is still available for your own planning and for the PDF.
